# Scene asset provenance

Downloaded 21 November 2026. All Poly Haven assets below are CC0 (commercial use and redistribution permitted): https://polyhaven.com/license

- Fern 02, Rico Cilliers / Rob Tuytel: https://polyhaven.com/a/fern_02 — glTF mesh, 1K colour, normal, packed material and alpha maps. Reused in small wall-edge plant clusters.
- Mossy Rock, Rob Tuytel: https://polyhaven.com/a/mossy_rock — 1K surface maps for irregular wall growth.
- Marble 01, Rob Tuytel: https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01 — 1K colour, normal and roughness maps for the paths.
- Old Stone Wall 02: https://polyhaven.com/a/old_stone_wall_02 — 1K maps for temple stonework.
- Moss 01: https://polyhaven.com/a/moss_01 — inspected during selection; not used in the live scene.

Vines, temple detailing, orb effects, architecture and atmosphere are custom geometry/shaders for this project. Exact emblem paths and stairs remain the existing Mantrium geometry.

The purchased SketchUp and 3ds Max collections were inspected. Their vegetation packs require a separate import/conversion pass; no proprietary library assets are included in this release.

Three.js and its GLTFLoader / BufferGeometryUtils are version 0.170.0, MIT licensed. See vendor/three.module.js and https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r170/LICENSE.

Foliage upgrade: Poly Haven Island Tree 02, CC0 (https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_02; https://polyhaven.com/license). Optimized mesh with photographic bark and leaf textures. Its textures are also used on custom curved climbing leaves and tapered wall roots. Original source retained in work/island-tree-original; optimized tree is 14.8 MB. Wind and orb gust deformation are original.
