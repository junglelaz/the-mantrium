THE MANTRIUM — ORACLE SKY CONTEXT (jyotisha124)

Astronomy Engine 2.1.19 by Don Cross, MIT:
https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy
https://pypi.org/project/astronomy-engine/2.1.19/
Seven physical bodies: apparent geocentric true-ecliptic longitudes, sampled every
three hours and interpolated along the shortest angular arc at the server's UTC.
The bundled ephemeris covers 1 January 2026 through 31 December 2036. Positions
are calculated astronomical data, not an external daily feed or AI guesses.
Regenerate/extend with work/astronomy/generate.py before 2037. Outside coverage,
the system explicitly omits sky claims; it never silently reuses an old date.

Approximate Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanamsa polynomial adapted from Grahan, MIT:
https://github.com/svarbhanu/grahan/blob/main/packages/vedic/src/ayanamsa.ts
Retains the mean-offset approximation with apparent longitudes. This is not an
exact ritual almanac. Mean lunar nodes use the standard mean ascending-node
polynomial; Ketu is opposite Rahu. No true-node or natal-chart claims are made.

Panchanga at the current instant:
tithi = floor(normalized Moon-Sun / 12 degrees) + 1;
nakshatra = floor(sidereal Moon / 13 1/3 degrees);
yoga = floor(normalized sidereal Moon+Sun / 13 1/3 degrees);
karana = successive six-degree elongation segments (fixed and repeating sequence).
Vara is the local weekday of the most recent sunrise. Sunrise uses PHP's
https://www.php.net/manual/en/function.date-sun-info.php with half-degree-rounded
IP-derived approximate coordinates and an IANA timezone. Coordinates are not
included in the model prompt. No reliable location or no recent polar sunrise:
vara is omitted. Current elements and sunrise elements are kept separate.

Validation: 13,860 sampled planetary comparisons over 2026–2036 against an
independent ephemeris, max sampled difference 26.25 arcseconds. This is sampled
validation, not a universal accuracy guarantee. A 0.04-degree boundary guard
avoids overconfident Panchanga labels close to transitions. No exact end-times,
festival dates, auspicious time windows or birth-chart readings are supplied.
Swiss Ephemeris was used only as a development comparison tool and is NOT
included in or required by this website.

Interpretation: Jyotisha themes are symbolic cultural/spiritual interpretations,
not established predictions of real-world geopolitical, medical or financial
outcomes. The Oracle may be poetic and forward-looking, but must not fabricate
positions, geographic forecasts, birth data or claims of supernatural certainty.

There are no astronomy API fees, API keys, third-party daily feed calls or cron
jobs. OpenAI dialogue still uses the existing API credits and request limits.
